Privacy Policy provides a clear, focused way to read the privacy policy and warranty information for StefanRonnkvist.com.

Review how information may be collected, used, and shared. Read about third-party services, the warranty disclaimer, and how policy changes are communicated. The effective date is displayed with the policy so you can identify the version you are reading.

A dedicated Help tab explains how to navigate the app, what the policy covers, and where third-party privacy practices apply.

Features:
- Complete policy in a clean, scrollable layout
- Effective date shown with the document
- Help and policy-scope guidance
- Responsive presentation for phones and larger screens
- No account or sign-in required

The policy and help text are bundled with the installed app. This app is a policy viewer; it does not create privacy policies or provide legal advice.
